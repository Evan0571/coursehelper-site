Something else on your mind?
Who is CourseHelper for?

Students who want their courses, class content, and review in one place. English and Chinese support is especially useful when you’re studying in English and thinking across both languages.

What can I keep in a course?

Class sessions, course details, files, links, and study conversations. Inside a lecture, you can work with its transcript, summary, notes, and materials, then create review content from that context.

How do recordings and AI features work?

You can record or import class audio when you have permission. Transcription and AI features depend on the selected service, connection, and available usage. You can check your plan and available usage in the app.

How can I try CourseHelper?

Get in touch for current availability and access information. Contact CourseHelper

Where can I find privacy and account information?
