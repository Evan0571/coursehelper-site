Something else on your mind?
Who is CourseHelper for?

Students who want their courses, class content, and review in one place. English and Chinese support is especially useful when you’re studying in English and thinking across both languages.

What can I keep in a course?

Class sessions, course details, files, links, and study conversations. Inside a lecture, you can work with its transcript, summary, notes, and materials, then create review content from that context.

Can I use a recording I already have?

Yes. With permission, import the audio into the class session or record there. On supported devices, on-device transcription keeps the audio on your phone and saves the text to your account. Cloud transcription uploads a copy and uses your cloud allowance.

How do I check an AI answer against my class materials?

When an answer cites your class materials, tap a numbered source to see what it refers to. If the original is available, open the note, transcript, or file to read it in context.
