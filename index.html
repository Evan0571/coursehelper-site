Sample lectureBIO 140

Enzymes & cellular energy

Key idea

Enzymes lower the energy needed to start a reaction. Its starting and ending energy stay the same.

Before the next class

Draw the energy curves with and without an enzyme.

00:30
Think of activation energy as the initial climb. An enzyme provides another, lower route without changing where you begin or end.
01:00
Substrates bind at the active site. After products leave, the enzyme can catalyze another reaction.

Sample quiz · BIO 1401 / 2

After an enzyme helps one reaction, what happens to it?

The products leave the active site, freeing the enzyme to work again.

Why might an enzyme work more slowly at a very high temperature?

Excessive heat can change the shape of the protein, making it harder for the substrate to bind.
